# Data sources and conventions

**Equities.** SPY, QQQ and IWM daily OHLC, adjusted close, volume and available actions
were retrieved through yfinance from Yahoo Finance for 2015-01-01–2025-12-31.
`auto_adjust=False`; adjusted close is explicit. The end request is exclusive
2026-01-01. Source files, row counts, retrieval metadata and SHA256 hashes are in
`data/raw/` and `data/download_manifest.json`. There are 2,766 exchange sessions per ETF.

Adjusted prices are treated as a reinvested-distribution proxy. Current vendor
history is not a point-in-time archive. Missing prices are not forward-filled;
calendar mismatches and invalid dates/prices/volume fail validation. Processed
log and simple returns are regenerated by `phase2.py` from the raw snapshot.

**Cash.** Federal Reserve Bank of New York historical EFFR; December 2014 is a
buffer for lagging rates at the start of the unchanged 2015–2025 ETF dataset.
`data/cash/effr.csv`, the raw response and manifest preserve provenance. Quotes
are made available conservatively two US federal business days after observation,
then must be available by the holding-start close. Annual percent /100 × actual
calendar days /360 defines the cash proxy return. This is an approximate deposit
accrual, not a directly investable EFFR instrument. Normal publication-day revisions
precede the lag; extraordinary later revisions cannot be ruled out without vintages.
Borrowing adds an assumed 100-bp spread, rather than historical broker contract data.

Official methods: [yfinance download](https://ranaroussi.github.io/yfinance/reference/api/yfinance.download.html),
[NY Fed EFFR](https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/reference-rates/effr),
[reference-rate publication and revisions](https://www.newyorkfed.org/markets/reference-rates/additional-information-about-reference-rates).

Third-party data retains its providers' terms. A code license, if added by the
owner, would not itself grant rights to redistribute third-party market data.
No financial data is synthesized or relabeled to fill gaps in this research.
